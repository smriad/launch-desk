# Hacktoberfest 2026 — participant announcement (draft)

**Headline:** Hacktoberfest 2026 — AI belongs to everyone

October is Hacktoberfest 2026 — a month-long, **free** celebration where **AI belongs to everyone**, with a focus on open-weight models and open-source AI. If you are a student or early-career developer curious about open source, you can learn and build online all October or connect at **300+ in-person Fests** worldwide.

Hacktoberfest is managed by **MLH and DEV** and presented by **DigitalOcean**. This year rewards are about participation, not pull requests: **PRs no longer count toward swag**, so lean into livestreams, weekly **DEV Challenges**, and **Global Hack Week** instead of chasing merge counts.

**Your next step:** Sign in with **MyMLH** at [Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest.com/) and join at least one online activity in October. Add your mailing address on the dashboard (opens by October 1), earn virtual stickers as you go, and unlock a mailed sticker pack after you collect three (shipping 8–12 weeks after the event). Connect your DEV account optionally for badges and challenge credit.

We are excited to welcome you this October.
